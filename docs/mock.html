Round 3, 2026-11-27 (newest)

  • Orientation follows the phone. Press "(mock) turn the phone" on the Live screen. The phone goes portrait and the OBS source turns into a 9:16 box that refits the canvas. Your scene isn't touched, only the source's own box changes.
  • Lock (on the phone's rail and in the dock) freezes the current shape. Turning the phone then changes nothing in OBS.
  • Mirror and Flip are on both sides, and the phone owns them like zoom, so pressing one on either side changes both screens and the Program.

Round 2, 2026-11-26

  • 0.65x works on your phone. The probe showed the phone only lists cameras 0 and 1, but a normal app can still open hidden camera 4. Asking for 0.65x got exactly 0.65x, and the phone switched to its ultrawide. Your Back camera is now 0.65x to 10x, with presets 0.65x, 1x and 2x.
  • Hidden by rule, not by name: cameras 0, 2 and 3 are already inside camera 4. Camera 5 maxes out at 1024x768, below 720p. Another phone gets the same rules applied to whatever it reports.
  • A, B and C are one dock. The ▭ button shows or hides the preview (A or B). Drag the title bar out, press ⧉ or double-click the title to snap it out (C). Drag it onto the right edge, or press ⧉ again, to snap it back. In the real plugin this is OBS's own dock behaviour.
  • Scanner: tap anywhere to focus (a ring appears and the picture sharpens), pinch or scroll to zoom, use the preset chips, and the torch is top-right.
