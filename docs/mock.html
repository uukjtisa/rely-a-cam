Round 5, 2026-11-27 (newest)

  • The phone's live screen fits now. Five icon buttons: Camera, Mic, Torch, Setup, Dark. Landscape puts them on the right; portrait puts them in one row at the bottom, next to Stop ("(mock) turn the phone" under the phone). Hover a button to see its name; on the phone you press and hold. Mirror, Flip and Lock turn moved into Setup > Picture.
  • One status line. "● Live · Wi-Fi · 1080p30 · 41°C". Pick 4K in Setup and it reads 49°C, goes yellow and says "hot: try 1080p" (the warning starts at 45°C). Codec, bitrate and battery moved into Setup > Video.
  • Slim zoom ruler on both sides: a thin line, a tick at each whole zoom, a label at each preset and at the top end, and a small white handle. Drag anywhere on it or click a label. The dock's preset buttons are gone; the ruler's labels do that job.
  • Tools. Click the phone's picture to focus: a ring and a ☀ brightness slider appear beside it for 3 s. Press and hold for AE/AF LOCK (the ring turns yellow). White balance and Snapshot to PC are in Setup > Picture; in the dock they're in ⋯ > Picture, with Snapshot also a button.
  • Overlays. Grid (thirds) has its own on/off for the dock, the phone and the OBS output (Setup > Overlays, or ⋯ > Overlays > Grid). Level line is phone-only. Detection appears beside Grid once Rely-A-Cam-Gestures is installed (tick it in the top bar).
  • The dock is lean. Chips, one status line, the preview, the ruler, and a row of icons: Mic (with its meter), Torch, Snapshot, ⋯. Everything else lives in the ⋯ menu, which is also what you get on right-click of the preview. Options hide while what they depend on is off: mute the mic and Input, Clean-up, Every scene and Use in other apps disappear from ⋯ > Mic.
  • One-click VB-Cable. ⋯ > Mic > Use in other apps... asks first, then runs the steps (download, signature check, install with Windows' own two prompts, defaults put back) and turns it on. With the cable installed (tick it in the top bar), the menu shows Use in other apps, "...and be Windows' default mic" (only while it's on), and Remove VB-Cable...
  • Android's status bar is hidden while live (it was never drawn in this mock; the real app hides it).

Round 4, 2026-11-27

  • Each phone's mic is its own OBS source, separate from its camera. In the Audio Mixer it's "Phone Mic - nova 10". With Every scene on (in the dock), it's heard in every scene, like OBS's own Mic/Aux, so switching to the other phone's camera never cuts your main mic. Turn it off and the mic becomes a normal source in this scene (see Sources).
  • The first phone you pair is the main mic: its mic is on and set to Every scene. Phones paired after it start with their mic off. Try it with "+ Add phone", then "Simulate a phone scanning".
  • Mic setup on both sides. In the dock: Input (only inputs the phone reports; a wired, USB or Bluetooth mic appears when it's connected), Clean-up (Noise, Echo, Auto gain, showing only what that phone has; Example B only has Noise), a live meter with a CLIP light, and Mic only. On the phone, tap the quality pill (⚙ Setup) for the same controls.
  • Mic only turns the camera off and keeps the mic streaming. The OBS source shows "camera off" and the phone shows a big meter. Auto gain keeps the meter out of the red, so CLIP stops lighting.
  • Use in other apps (added after approval) lets Chrome, Discord or Zoom use the phone mic. Windows only sees an OBS source as a mic through a virtual cable, so this plays the mic into the free VB-Audio Virtual Cable and those apps pick "CABLE Output". If the cable isn't installed, the button opens its download page instead.
  • Volume, filters, monitoring and sync offset stay in OBS's own Audio Mixer, so the dock doesn't copy them.

Round 3, 2026-11-27

  • Orientation follows the phone. Press "(mock) turn the phone" on the Live screen. The phone goes portrait and the OBS source turns into a 9:16 box that refits the canvas. Your scene isn't touched, only the source's own box changes.
  • Lock (on the phone's rail and in the dock) freezes the current shape. Turning the phone then changes nothing in OBS.
  • Mirror and Flip are on both sides, and the phone owns them like zoom, so pressing one on either side changes both screens and the Program.

Round 2, 2026-11-26

  • 0.65x works on your phone. The probe showed the phone only lists cameras 0 and 1, but a normal app can still open hidden camera 4. Asking for 0.65x got exactly 0.65x, and the phone switched to its ultrawide. Your Back camera is now 0.65x to 10x, with presets 0.65x, 1x and 2x.
  • Hidden by rule, not by name: cameras 0, 2 and 3 are already inside camera 4. Camera 5 maxes out at 1024x768, below 720p. Another phone gets the same rules applied to whatever it reports.
  • A, B and C are one dock. The ▭ button shows or hides the preview (A or B). Drag the title bar out, press ⧉ or double-click the title to snap it out (C). Drag it onto the right edge, or press ⧉ again, to snap it back. In the real plugin this is OBS's own dock behaviour.
  • Scanner: tap anywhere to focus (a ring appears and the picture sharpens), pinch or scroll to zoom, use the preset chips, and the torch is top-right.

Round 1, 2026-11-26 P1P2P3NEW

  • Try the sync. Scroll over the dock preview (mouse wheel), or hold Ctrl and scroll, which is what a touchpad pinch sends on Windows. You can also drag the slider or press + / - / 1-9. Scroll over the phone screen to "pinch" there. Both screens follow, and the phone owns the number.
  • Feel the lag Sonnet's draft had. Set Network to 300 ms, untick PC prediction, and scroll the dock. Then tick it back. The dock now moves instantly, but the Program preview (what viewers get) only moves once the phone has really zoomed. The dashed outline on the dock's zoom number is a mock-only marker for "showing the prediction".
  • Nothing hardcoded. Switch Phone 1 between nova 10, Example A and Example B. Presets, slider ends, camera list, torch and quality list all come from the phone's own report. Example B's front camera can't zoom at all, and both sides say so.
